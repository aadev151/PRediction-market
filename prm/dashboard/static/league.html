Brier score by method

Brier = mean squared error of the probability vs. the outcome (0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip). Brackets are bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. "Best single trader" is picked after the fact, and the base rate uses this run's own outcomes, so both are optimistic baselines.

Calibration

Each bubble is a 0.1-wide price bin, sized by how many markets fell in it. On the dashed diagonal, "0.8" really means 80%.

Trader bankrolls

Proper scoring pays traders who are right and calibrated. Every trader starts at the same bankroll.
